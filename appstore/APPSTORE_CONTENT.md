## Description (4000 characters max)

**Your photos. Your documents. Your AI. No cloud required.**

0711 Vault is a privacy-first photo and document vault with a local AI assistant that learns about YOUR life — without ever sending your data to external servers.

**🔐 TRUE PRIVACY**
Unlike iCloud or Google Photos, your data stays on YOUR devices and YOUR servers. We use military-grade AES-256 encryption. Not even we can see your photos. Because we never have access.

**🧠 AI THAT'S ACTUALLY YOURS**
Our built-in AI assistant runs 100% locally. Ask questions like:
• "When did I last see Mom?"
• "Show me photos from my Italy trip"
• "Find my insurance documents"
• "Who was at the Christmas party?"

The AI learns from YOUR photos and documents — not millions of other users. Your memories, your assistant, your privacy.

**📸 SMART PHOTO ORGANIZATION**
• Automatic face recognition (on-device)
• Semantic search — find photos by describing them
• Smart albums generated by AI
• "On This Day" memories from years past
• Location and timeline organization

**📄 DOCUMENT VAULT**
• Store receipts, contracts, IDs securely
• OCR text extraction for searchability
• Automatic categorization
• Never lose an important document again

**🔄 OPTIONAL SELF-HOSTED SYNC**
Want access from multiple devices? Sync to YOUR OWN server. We provide easy Docker deployment. Your data center, your rules.
