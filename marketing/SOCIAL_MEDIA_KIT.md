## LinkedIn Post

**For Founders/Tech Audience:**

"We just shipped 0711 Vault — a photo app with a fundamental difference.

The AI runs locally.

Not "processes locally then uploads." Not "encrypts before cloud." Actually runs on the user's device. Period.

Why does this matter?

Because the dirty secret of "AI-powered" photo apps is that your photos become training data. Your family's faces teach their models. Your home address refines their location AI.

We decided: what if we just... didn't do that?

The result:
- Face recognition that never phones home
- Semantic search ("beach photos from summer") on-device
- An AI assistant you can ask "when did I last see Mom?" without that query going to a server

Is it harder to build? Yes.
Is it more expensive to run? No — the user's device does the work.
Is it the right thing to do? Absolutely.

Try it: vault.0711.io
Self-host it: github.com/christoph-ui/0711-Vault

Privacy shouldn't be a premium feature. It should be the default."
